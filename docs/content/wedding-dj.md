## A full evening, or the perfect follow-on.

### Full-evening wedding DJ

Choosing a DJ as your main evening entertainment? Plan a night that moves through the music you love, with room for the songs your guests will join you on the floor for. We'll discuss your first dance, the feel of the evening and how you want the night to finish.

### After-band DJ sets

Already booked a band? Keep the celebration moving with a DJ set afterwards. Share the band's finish time and your venue details so we can plan the handover and agree the right setup and playing times.

Both options are quoted for your event. An enquiry lets us check availability and discuss the details; it doesn't reserve your date.

## Music that feels like you.

Bring the songs that mean something to you: the first dance, a favourite from a festival, a track your friends always sing, or the ones you'd rather leave out.

The LEDJS wedding service includes a pre-wedding consultation and playlist planning. Use that conversation to explain your tastes and your crowd, while leaving room for your DJ to respond to the atmosphere on the night.

## The practical details, planned together.

Professional sound, lighting and MC services form part of the wedding offering. We discuss the venue, event schedule and announcements with you so the quote is clear about what's included.

Ask about access and setup times, your band's equipment arrangements if applicable, any venue sound restrictions and the agreed finish time. These details belong in the plan before the day arrives.

## Want to give the dancefloor another dimension?

Add synchronised LED wristbands to your wedding DJ experience. Coordinated colours and effects bring guests into the light show, creating a shared moment for a song or finale you want to remember.

Wristbands are an enhancement to the music-led celebration. Tell us your ideas and we'll discuss options, quantities and costs as part of your quote.

## Wedding DJ questions

### Can we book a DJ for the whole evening?

Yes. LEDJS offers full-evening wedding DJ entertainment as well as after-band sets. Tell us which you're considering and the timings you have in mind.

### Can we share must-play and do-not-play songs?

Yes. Playlist planning is part of the wedding service. Share the songs that matter to you, your first dance and anything you don't want played.

### What affects the quote?

The date, venue, playing times, agreed sound and lighting setup, travel and any enhancements all help define the package. Send your details for a tailored quote rather than relying on a price for a different event.

### Do you cover our county?

We welcome enquiries from Longford, Cavan, Leitrim, Westmeath, Meath, Mayo, Galway, Dublin and Offaly. Share the venue so travel and practical arrangements can be included in the discussion.

### Are wristbands compulsory?

No. Book the wedding DJ experience that suits you. Wristbands are an optional enhancement to discuss if you want that added visual moment.

### Does checking a date confirm a booking?

No. Availability, the final package and booking terms must be agreed with LEDJS. Your enquiry starts that conversation.

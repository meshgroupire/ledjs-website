# Songo Music Bingo with LEDJS

LEDJS is a Songo franchisee. Planning a club fundraiser, staff gathering or venue night? Talk to Dave about bringing Songo to your crowd.

## Recognise the tune. Join in the game.

Song titles take the place of numbers on the bingo cards. Listen, mark the songs as they play and follow the host's instructions for each round. On-screen song information helps the room take part.

## Plan an event that fits your crowd.

Start with your date, venue and expected attendance. We can discuss whether the format suits your group, the music you have in mind and how the evening will run.

For a fundraiser, talk through your ticket plan, organiser roles and prize budget before promoting the event. For a staff or venue night, agree the schedule and practical setup in advance.

## The details to agree before booking

Songo's published information describes a standard format of four rounds plus DJ entertainment. Your LEDJS quote will confirm the timings, equipment, prize arrangements and total cost for your event.

You can read the [official Songo event information](https://songo.ie/info/) and discuss the arrangements with Dave.

## Useful questions

### Who do I contact about a Songo event?

Contact Dave at LEDJS to discuss your date, venue and event plans.

### What should we include in an enquiry?

Your date, venue or town, type of event and approximate attendance are a useful start. Mention if you are fundraising and any venue constraints or timings you already know.

### What kind of venue works?

Tell us about your venue, expected attendance and screen or projector access. We will discuss the setup and any equipment requirements as part of your quote.

Start with the practical stuff.

Can we mix a DJ set with music bingo?

Yes. LEDJS offers music bingo, speed quizzing, karaoke and DJ entertainment. Tell us about your crowd and schedule, and we'll discuss a combination that fits.

Do you travel outside Longford?

LEDJS is based in Longford and offers event entertainment across Ireland. Share your venue or town so travel, timings and any additional costs can be included in your quote.

Can we choose the music for our wedding?

The existing wedding service includes a planning consultation and a custom playlist. Share the songs you love and the ones you'd rather leave out, then discuss the flow of the night with your DJ.

Does an enquiry reserve our date?

No. An enquiry starts a conversation. Availability, the final package and booking terms need to be confirmed with LEDJS.

Can I buy the tickets shown here?

These are illustrative events in a design proposal. No tickets are on sale and no payments can be taken. The proposed live experience would show confirmed venues, complete prices and refund terms before checkout.

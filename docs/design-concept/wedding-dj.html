Wedding DJ questions

Can we book a DJ for the whole evening?

Yes. LEDJS offers full-evening wedding DJ entertainment as well as after-band sets. Tell us which you're considering and the timings you have in mind.

Can we share must-play and do-not-play songs?

Yes. Playlist planning is part of the wedding service. Share the songs that matter to you, your first dance and anything you don't want played.

What affects the quote?

The date, venue, playing times, agreed sound and lighting setup, travel and any enhancements all help define the package. Send your details for a tailored quote rather than relying on a price for a different event.

Do you cover our county?

We welcome enquiries from Longford, Cavan, Leitrim, Westmeath, Meath, Mayo, Galway, Dublin and Offaly. Share the venue so travel and practical arrangements can be included in the discussion.

Are wristbands compulsory?

No. Book the wedding DJ experience that suits you. Wristbands are an optional enhancement to discuss if you want that added visual moment.

Does checking a date confirm a booking?

No. Availability, the final package and booking terms must be agreed with LEDJS. Your enquiry starts that conversation.
